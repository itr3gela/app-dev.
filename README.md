# The Walking Dead

*The Walking Dead* is an American post-apocalyptic horror television series based on the comic book series by **Robert Kirkman**, **Tony Moore**, and **Charlie Adlard**.

## Main Themes
- Survival in a zombie-infested world
- Human relationships and conflict
- Morality and leadership

## Key Characters
1. **Rick Grimes** – Former sheriff's deputy and group leader
2. **Daryl Dixon** – Skilled hunter and tracker
3. **Michonne** – Sword-wielding warrior
4. **Carol Peletier** – From abuse survivor to powerful leader

> _“We are the walking dead.” – Rick Grimes_

## Notable Facts
- Premiered: *October 31, 2010*
- Network: AMC
- Total Seasons: 11
- Spin-offs include: _Fear the Walking Dead_, _The Walking Dead: Daryl Dixon_, and _The Walking Dead: Dead City_
